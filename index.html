Everyday use

ClipSearch is not capturing new clips

Open ClipSearch from the menu bar. Check that capture is not paused, Private Mode is off, and the source app is not excluded in Preferences. Then copy a short plain-text item in TextEdit and reopen the history.

Some apps restrict clipboard access. Sensitive-content settings may also exclude a clip. Avoid testing with a password or other secret.

Find and reuse a clip

Open the menu-bar panel and search by content, title, tag, or file path. Select a result to copy it back to the system clipboard, then paste in the destination app. Compact mode shows more items; verbose mode includes metadata and actions.

Move history to another Mac

Export a .clipsearch archive, transfer it securely, and import it on the other Mac. Import merges with existing history instead of replacing it. File references contain paths, not the original files; those paths may not exist on the new Mac.

Restore deleted items or a backup

Open Recently Deleted to restore items before they are permanently removed. For a verified database backup, use the storage and backup section in Preferences. Restoration creates a safety backup before replacing the current database.

Exports and backups are separate copies. Deleting an item from history does not delete it from an older archive or backup.

iCloud sync

Cloud availability depends on the installed build. Local-only builds do not offer sync.

Enable sync or diagnose a paused connection

In a cloud-enabled build, sync requires Pro, explicit opt-in, the same iCloud account on both Macs, and Passwords & Keychain enabled in iCloud settings. Start in the cloud section of Preferences. ClipSearch makes a local safety backup before initial enablement.

When the original encryption key has not arrived through iCloud Keychain, sync waits. Keep both Macs signed in and retry later. Do not reset Keychain or delete cloud data to troubleshoot. If the original key is lost, an independent export or backup may be needed.

What happens when I turn off sync or Pro expires?

Transfers pause and local history stays accessible. Existing cloud records are not erased. While sync is active, supported changes and deletions propagate to other devices. File permissions, backups, app exclusions, and device settings do not sync.

Purchases and tips

Manage or restore ClipSearch Pro

Pro is offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription, not a lifetime purchase. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy it. Manage or cancel renewal through Apple's subscription settings.

History remains free. The free creation limits are 3 collections, 3 saved searches, and 10 snippets. Existing local content remains accessible after Pro expires.

Are tips required? How are they charged?

Tips are optional, one-time App Store purchases and unlock no features. The app shows the available amounts in your storefront's currency before Apple's confirmation. A tip is not a subscription.

For an eligible refund request, use Apple's Report a Problem service.
