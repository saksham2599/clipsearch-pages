ClipSearch Privacy Policy

Local-first clipboard history, with optional Apple services.

Effective September 7, 2026

Who is responsible

ClipSearch is developed by Saksham Goyal. For privacy questions or help managing your data, email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com.

Clipboard history on your Mac

When capture is enabled, ClipSearch stores clipboard items made available by macOS. Depending on your settings and app version, this can include text, links, rich text, images, colors, file references, titles, tags, snippets, collections, saved searches, source-app information, timestamps, and paste-queue entries.

History is stored in the app's local database. Images are stored as local files, with metadata in the database. File clips store paths or security-scoped references, not copies of the original files.

You can pause capture, use Private Mode, exclude applications, delete history, and configure retention. Sensitive-content detection is heuristic and may miss secrets. Recently Deleted, local backups, and exports may retain separate copies until you remove them.

No advertising or tracking SDKs

ClipSearch does not send clipboard history to a developer-operated server. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use clipboard content for advertising or tracking. Optional iCloud sync and App Store purchases communicate with Apple as described below.

Exports and local backups

Exports are explicit user actions. Backups follow your backup settings; ClipSearch also creates safety backups for protected operations such as restoring a database or enabling cloud sync. Archives may include selected clipboard items, metadata, and image payloads. Import merges with the history already on your Mac.

You control where exports are shared. Treat archives and backups as sensitive files; do not assume they have the same encryption as cloud records. Deleting history does not erase existing exported files or backups.

Optional iCloud sync

In builds that include cloud sync, it requires Pro and explicit opt-in and is off by default. ClipSearch encrypts supported records on your Mac before uploading them to your private CloudKit database. Records may contain clip contents, images, rich text, titles, tags, snippets, collections, saved searches, and metadata needed to merge changes.

File paths synchronize, but the original files and security-scoped permissions do not. Device settings, exclusions, local backups, paste queues, and purchase receipts are not part of the clipboard sync payload. Apple processes service metadata needed to operate iCloud.

The encryption key is stored in synchronizable iCloud Keychain items. A non-secret key identifier and keyed verification value coordinate access between your devices. ClipSearch pauses if the original key is missing or does not match. Losing access to the key can make the cloud copy unreadable. Keep a separate backup or export of important history.

Turning off sync or letting Pro expire pauses transfers but does not erase existing cloud data. While sync is active, deletions propagate; deletion markers may remain to prevent an older device from restoring deleted entries. Backups and exports are independent copies.

App Store purchases

Apple processes Pro subscriptions and optional tips. ClipSearch uses verified StoreKit transaction information to determine access and restore purchases. The app does not collect payment-card details or send receipts to a developer-operated server. Apple may provide the developer with sales and financial reports through its services.

Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account. See Apple's App Store privacy notice for Apple's handling of purchase data.

Support and this website

If you contact support, the email and any information you choose to include are used to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send secrets or unredacted clipboard content. You can ask for deletion of your support correspondence; some records may need to be retained where required by law.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or third-party fonts. GitHub may process connection information to serve and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement. External links are governed by the destination service's policies.

Security and your choices

Cloud encryption does not mean the local SQLite database or every export is app-level encrypted. Local protection relies on macOS account access controls and, when enabled, FileVault. ClipSearch validates imports and verifies backups, but no clipboard manager can guarantee that storing secrets is safe.

You can manage captured data and retention in Preferences. For help with deleting local or cloud copies, contact support before resetting app storage or Keychain. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove exported archives, backups, or cloud records.

Policy updates

This policy may change as ClipSearch changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in the policy and relevant app disclosures.
